Gaurav Harsh is a senior UX designer and researcher in Bengaluru with six years in B2B and enterprise SaaS. At ServiceNow he runs research across three product teams and has led 14 focus groups with enterprise customers that changed what got built. Before that, at Acuity Knowledge Partners, he designed three SaaS products and ran mixed-method studies with more than 50 participants. He began at MakeMyTrip in 2020, where a heuristic review cut the bounce rate on a critical screen by 20%. For over two years he has worked daily with Claude and Claude Code alongside Figma, for synthesis, prototyping and handoff. He holds a Master of Design from NIFT. Say hello.
